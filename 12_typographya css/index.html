clean mahatrashtra compaign

The Maharashtra government has continued cleanliness and sanitation initiatives under the broader Swachh Bharat Mission, with campaigns promoting waste management, public hygiene, and citizen participation.

On 1 March 2026, a major cleanliness drive was held at Maharashtra Lok Bhavan. Around 200 volunteers collected 416 kg of plastic and thermocol waste from the premises and nearby shoreline.

Several municipal bodies in Maharashtra have also organized local clean-up campaigns. For example, Nagpur Municipal Corporation launched a large-scale park clean-up drive with participation from residents, NGOs, and students.
